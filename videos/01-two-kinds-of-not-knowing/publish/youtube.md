# YouTube

**Title:** Two kinds of not knowing | Uncertainty Explainers 1

**Description:**

Three people say "I do not know": one about to roll a die, one holding a sealed envelope, one asked the price of copper twenty years from now. When someone says they do not know, what exactly is it they do not know, and does the difference matter? Two questions sort uncertainty: is it chance or ignorance, and can it be measured? Four stops answer them: a die and an envelope (aleatory and epistemic uncertainty), Frank Knight's fire insurance and John Maynard Keynes's "We simply do not know" (risk and true uncertainty), Daniel Ellsberg's two urns (ambiguity), and a scale from complete certainty to total ignorance.
