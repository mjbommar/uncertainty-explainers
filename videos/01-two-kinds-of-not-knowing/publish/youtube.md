**Title:** Two kinds of not knowing | Uncertainty Explainers 1

**Description:**

Some uncertainty is chance and some is ignorance. Some can be measured and some cannot. The first job of a forecast is to say which kind it faces. A die and a sealed envelope, Frank Knight's fire insurance, John Maynard Keynes's "We simply do not know", Daniel Ellsberg's two urns, and a scale that runs from complete certainty to total ignorance.
