# YouTube: From evidence to forecast

## Title

From evidence to forecast | Uncertainty Explainers 2

## Description

A positive screening test. Ninety-five of a hundred physicians put the chance of cancer at 70 to 80 percent; the right answer is 7.8 percent. Where does a probability come from, and how can you tell whether it is any good?

A probability is a summary of evidence, not a fact about the world. Five stops: what the number counts (Hájek's reference class, Laplace's sunrise), how evidence should move it (Bayes' rule with 1,000 women), when one number is too many (the unseen coin, Ellsberg's urn), what it can say about the future (Hume, Lorenz and the weather ensemble; forecast, projection and scenario), and how we know it was good (the Brier score, calibration, overconfidence).
