# YouTube: From evidence to forecast

## Title

From evidence to forecast | Uncertainty Explainers 2

## Description

A probability in a forecast is not a fact about the world. It is a summary of evidence: honest about the group it counts over, honest about how wide it is, and stated so it can be scored. This video walks from Laplace's sunrise and the cube factory, through Bayes' rule with 1,000 women, Ellsberg's urn and Dempster-Shafer belief, Hume's problem of induction, Lorenz and the weather ensemble, the difference between a forecast, a projection, a scenario and a counterfactual, to the Brier score and calibration.

Next in the series: Saying it out loud.
