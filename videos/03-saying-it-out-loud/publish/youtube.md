# YouTube: Saying it out loud

**Title:** Saying it out loud | Uncertainty Explainers 3

**Description:**

In 1961 the Joint Chiefs called the Bay of Pigs plan "a fair chance". The officer who wrote it meant about three in ten; readers heard approval. This explainer follows the same failure through Sherman Kent's 1951 "serious possibility", the intelligence and climate ladders where "likely" means two different ranges, the "30% chance of rain", the hurricane cone and Hurricane Charley, the 2016 election forecasts, and the words accountants and courts use in place of numbers. The fix: pair every probability word with a number, a reference class, and a statement of confidence.

Third of three. 1: Two kinds of not knowing. 2: From evidence to forecast.
