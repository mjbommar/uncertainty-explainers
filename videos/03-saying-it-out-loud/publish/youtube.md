# YouTube: Saying it out loud

**Title:** Saying it out loud | Uncertainty Explainers 3

**Description:**

In 1961 the Joint Chiefs called the Bay of Pigs plan "a fair chance". The officer who wrote it meant about three in ten; the readers heard approval. Where, between the writer and the reader, did seven in ten disappear?

This explainer follows the message through six stops. The word: Sherman Kent's 1951 "serious possibility", and the intelligence and climate ladders where "likely" means two different ranges. The number's group: what "30% chance of rain" counts over. The picture: the hurricane cone and Hurricane Charley. The single result: the 2016 election forecasts. Words on purpose: the likelihood words accountants and courts use in place of numbers. The whole statement: the word, the number, the group, and how good the evidence is.

Third of three. 1: Two kinds of not knowing. 2: From evidence to forecast.
